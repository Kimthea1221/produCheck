ProduCheck

Version 1.0.0

ProduCheck is a thesis prototype Chrome extension that helps you check whether a cosmetic product on Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, or Facebook Marketplace appears in a list of FDA products. Results are a guide only, not an official FDA decision.

How it Works

3 simple steps:

Open

Browse cosmetic products on Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, or Facebook Marketplace. When you open a product page, a Check Product button appears.

Check

Click Check Product. ProduCheck reads the product title from the part of the page you can see and compares it with its FDA product list.

See the result

The product is shown as Registered, Unregistered, or Suspicious (we couldn't match it, so it needs further checking on the FDA portal).

Revisit reminder: If you open a product page again that was Unregistered or Suspicious when you checked it, ProduCheck shows a reminder of your earlier result. It does not check the product again by itself. Click Check Product to get the latest result.

Partner Agencies

The FDA is the only agency that sees complaints sent through ProduCheck. The CIDG only helps the FDA with taking down online stores or products when needed, and does not see complaints sent from the extension. ProduCheck is a prototype, so these complaints are not official reports.
