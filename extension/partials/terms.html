Thank you for trying ProduCheck! This page explains, in plain words, what ProduCheck is, what information it uses, and what you can expect from us. Please read it before you use the extension.

1. What ProduCheck is

ProduCheck is a thesis prototype made by a group of students at Pampanga State University. It helps online shoppers see whether a cosmetic product on Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, or Facebook Marketplace appears in a list of FDA products. It is a student project. It is not an official service of the FDA or any other government agency.

2. Please read this first: results and complaints are not official

  • Results are only a guide. They are not an official FDA decision about any product or seller.
  • Complaints sent through ProduCheck are not official reports. ProduCheck results and complaints cannot be used as evidence in court, or as a real report to the FDA or any other agency.
  • If you want to make an official report, please contact the FDA directly: hotline (02) 8857-1900 (cosmetics: local 8113), email info@fda.gov.ph, Region III office rfoiii@fda.gov.ph (San Fernando, Pampanga), or the FDA Verification Portal.
  • Our list of registered cosmetic products comes from information the FDA released to us through a Freedom of Information (eFOI) request. It has 93,837 entries, and the notification validity dates in the list run from 8 September 2026 to 26 March 2027. We received it on 28 April 2026. The list may be incomplete or out of date, ProduCheck matches products by name, and the AI that reads the picture can misread a title, so ProduCheck can make mistakes.

3. What the results mean

  • Registered: ProduCheck found a close match in its list of registered cosmetic products. This does not guarantee that the product is safe or genuine.
  • Unregistered: the title matches an entry in the FDA's public advisories about unregistered cosmetic products. Our copy has 6,301 entries, dated 23 November 2021 to 19 August 2026, taken from the FDA's advisories page (fda.gov.ph/advisories). This is not a legal finding. It does not prove that a product is fake or unsafe, and a product that is not on the list is not proven to be registered.
  • Suspicious: ProduCheck could not match the listing to a product in either list. This does not mean the product is unregistered or unsafe. Our lists may not cover every FDA-notified product, the listing title may differ from the registered name, or the item may not be a cosmetic product. Please check it on the FDA Verification Portal.

4. Why we collect information

We collect and use information only for educational purposes, specifically for our thesis: testing how well ProduCheck works and writing about the results. We will not use your information for anything that could cause you harm. We will not sell it or use it for advertising.

5. What ProduCheck uses, and when

When you click Check Product

  • No picture is taken until you click Check Product (or type a product name and confirm it). The only thing sent without a click is the revisit re-check, described under “What ProduCheck remembers on your device.” It sends a saved product title, never a picture.
  • ProduCheck takes a picture of the part of the shopping page you can see in your current browser tab. It does not capture your whole screen, your other tabs, or other apps. It hides its own button and pop-up first.
  • Anything visible in that part of the page can end up in the picture, for example your shop account name, a chat window, or a message preview. Please close chats, messages, and other personal information that may be visible on screen before you click Check Product.
  • The picture, the page address, and the shop name (such as Shopee or Lazada) are sent to our server. Our server passes the picture to an outside AI service (Anthropic's Claude), which reads the product title and store name from it, and the brand name when it is clearly visible on the product photo. The AI only reads the text. It does not decide the result. Our own system compares the title with our lists. We use the picture only for this.
  • Our server does not save the picture used for checking. It only holds it in memory while the details are being read. ProduCheck does not keep it on your device either. (A screenshot you choose to attach to a complaint is different: it is saved with the complaint, as explained below.)
  • The outside AI service follows its own data-handling rules, and we cannot control how long it keeps the information it receives.

If you type a product name instead

  • The name you type is sent to our server to be compared with our lists.

If you use ProduCheck without an account (as a guest)

  • You can check products as a guest. A guest check is saved without any link to an account. We keep the shop, the product title, the result, and the time.

If you create an account

  • We keep your email address, your username, and your password. Your password is stored in scrambled (hashed) form. We send you a one-time code to confirm your email, using Gmail's email service (Google).
  • While you are signed in, we save the products you check and their results, so you can see your verification history.

If you send a complaint

  • You need an account to send one.
  • We keep the product name, the link, the store name, the description you write, the screenshot if you attach one, and the result ProduCheck showed. It is saved to your account so you can follow its status.
  • Only the FDA sees complaints sent from the extension. The FDA asks the PNP-CIDG for help only when an online store or product must be taken down. The CIDG does not see complaints sent from the extension.
  • We cannot promise that a complaint will be reviewed, answered, or lead to any action.
  • Complaints stay on file even if you later delete your account, so please do not put personal details, such as your phone number or home address, in the description or in a screenshot you attach.

What ProduCheck remembers on your device

  • ProduCheck saves a few things in your Chrome browser, not on our server: a product ID taken from the page address of each product you checked, the product title, and its result; the full page address and title of the last product you checked; whether the Check Product button is switched on, that you agreed to these Terms, and your sign-in details if you are signed in.
  • This is how the revisit reminder works. When you open or return to a product page on a supported shop, ProduCheck looks for it in this saved list on your device. If you checked it before and its result was Unregistered or Suspicious, ProduCheck sends the saved product title (no picture) to our server to check it again, and Chrome shows a notification. If the product is now Registered, the notification says so. Otherwise, it shows your earlier or updated result. This happens only for product pages you already checked, and only after you agree to these Terms. You can click Check Product at any time for the full result.
  • ProduCheck does not keep a list of the pages you browse. It only remembers products you chose to check.
  • This information stays on your device until you remove the extension from Chrome.

ProduCheck’s Chrome permissions cover all websites, but it only acts on Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, and Facebook Marketplace product pages. It takes a picture only when you click Check Product.

Our server also records technical details, such as the time of a request and your IP address. We keep them until we submit the final thesis paper, and then delete them.

6. Who can see your information

  • Complaints: authorized FDA personnel see complaints sent from the extension. They read and update them in the interagency desktop application.
  • The rest of the information stored by the system can be accessed only by the members of our research team.
  • Service providers that handle part of your information: Anthropic (the AI service that reads the picture), Google (Gmail, which sends the one-time codes), and our hosting provider, Contabo (a virtual private server).
  • Our thesis paper may report results from the study, for example how many products were checked or how often each result appeared. It will not include your name, email, or other personal details.

7. How long we keep your information

All data collected while we developed and tested ProduCheck will be deleted by the members of our research team after we submit the final thesis paper. This includes accounts, checks, complaints, saved complaint screenshots, technical details, and backups. We may keep it until then because we may still need it for revisions after the final defense. The extension and the server may stay online after the study, but the data collected during the study will be deleted.

8. Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, and it travels over a secure (https) connection. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee complete protection.

9. Deleting your account

  • To delete your account: sign in, open the profile button at the top of any ProduCheck page, choose Delete Account, enter your password, and confirm. This cannot be undone.
  • Your email, username, and password are removed from our system. Your verification history and the complaints you already sent stay on file, but they are no longer connected to your email or username.
  • The information saved on your device stays until you remove the extension from Chrome.

10. Your choices and your rights

  • You are asked to agree to these Terms the first time you use ProduCheck, whether or not you have an account. Until you agree, ProduCheck stays switched off. If you choose "No, thanks", nothing is checked, captured, or sent. Your choice is saved on your device.
  • You can stop using ProduCheck at any time by removing the extension from Chrome.
  • We handle your personal information with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) in mind. Under this law, you have the right to be informed about how your information is used, to ask to see it, to ask us to correct it or delete it, and to withdraw your consent. To use these rights, email us (see Contact below).
